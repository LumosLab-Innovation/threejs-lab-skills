# Human Anatomy model attribution

The specimen GLBs and organ thumbnails under `specimens/` and `thumbs/` are adapted from [thebuggeddev/anatomy](https://github.com/thebuggeddev/anatomy).

They are included in this Lab029s non-commercial demo with direct permission from the repository author, as confirmed by the project owner on 2026-08-09. The upstream repository does not currently publish a standalone license file, so these assets must not be reused outside that permission without checking with the author.

Lab029s changes: Vite integration, Lab029s visual tokens, Vietnamese learning content, five-stage lesson flow, reduced-motion behavior, DPR cap, route cleanup, and lazy loading of one organ specimen at a time.

The Inner Ear specimen was generated for this Lab029s demo with Tripo and then fitted to the same runtime scale, material, loading, and annotation pipeline as the other Anatomy specimens. Confirm the applicable Tripo asset terms before transferring or reusing it commercially.
